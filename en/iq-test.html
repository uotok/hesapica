    METHOD AND SCIENTIFIC LIMITS

    What does this activity measure?

    It counts correct responses in four types of reasoning task. It does not produce a valid IQ score, mental age, diagnosis or clinical evaluation. Question content differs by age band, but this is not age norming.

    Transparent scoring

    Session accuracy = (correct answers ÷ total questions) × 100. Unanswered questions receive no credit. Category charts summarize correct responses from this session only. Time limits are not psychometrically standardized.

    Experimental IQ-style game score: This appears only for adults who answered at least 80% of the questions. Its artificial ceiling is 120 for 10 questions, 145 for 25 questions, and 160 for 40 questions or the advanced 20-question mode. These numbers are not measured IQ values, are not age-normed, and cannot be compared across modes. The results show the full formula.

    Who can use it?

    People aged eight or older can use this for learning and practice. Adult guidance is recommended for ages 8–17. This version is not appropriate under age eight. Psychologists may examine the educational examples, but results must not be used for diagnosis, intellectual classification or educational placement.

    What is different from professional testing?

    Valid psychological measurement needs age-appropriate normative samples, qualified administration, studies of reliability, validity, fairness and uncertainty. These have not been completed for this activity. Repeat attempts may benefit from practice effects.

    Privacy

    No name, school, date of birth or health details are requested. Answers are scored on your device; session results are not automatically saved. Sharing only happens after you request it. Review the site privacy policy for general analytics and cookie/ad practices.

    About this test

    Why does my result vary?

    Each run generates fresh question variants and balances the four reasoning categories. Current item difficulties are preliminary, not calibrated against a representative population.

    Can this replace a professional IQ assessment?

    No. Different age bands receive different types of question, but there are no population norms. This activity cannot establish intelligence level or a diagnosis.

    Does the experimental IQ-style number tell me my real intelligence?

    No. This 70–160 game indicator is derived from provisional question-design weights, without age norms or established measurement error. Values such as 130 or 160 have no psychometric IQ meaning. Do not use these results for intelligence labels, diagnosis or selection.

    Are results stored on this device?

    No automatic result history is saved in this version. Copying and sharing occur only when you choose to do so.
