    How this daily horoscope works

    Hesapica does not rotate through twelve prewritten horoscope paragraphs. For the selected date it computes a 12:00 UTC sky snapshot: tropical ecliptic longitudes for the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, their apparent retrograde status, and the major transit aspects between them.

    If you enter a Rising Sign, that sign becomes the 1st house in a Whole Sign house model. Without a Rising Sign, the tool uses a simpler solar-house model that treats the Sun sign as the 1st house. This is not a full natal chart; it is a transparent way to personalize daily life-area themes without asking for sensitive birth details.

    Astronomical calculation layer

    The Sun and planet positions use the approximate Keplerian orbital elements published by NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics for 1800–2050. A compact periodic lunar model is used for the Moon. The page does not depend on a third-party astrology API at runtime.

    Precision note: This layer is designed for a daily astrology reading; it is not a high-precision ephemeris or professional natal-chart engine. When a planet is extremely close to a sign boundary or a retrograde/direct station, the approximate model can differ slightly from a high-precision ephemeris.

    Important: Astrology is not scientifically established as a method for predicting the future. The planetary positions are astronomical calculations; assigning love, career, money or personality meaning to those positions is an astrological interpretation. Use the reading for entertainment and personal reflection.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this daily horoscope randomly generated?

    No. The same Sun sign, Rising Sign and date use the same astronomical sky positions and the same rule-based interpretation model; the result does not select random horoscope text.

    Do I have to enter my Rising Sign?

    No. Without a Rising Sign, the calculator uses a simplified solar-house approach that treats the Sun sign as the 1st house. Adding a Rising Sign personalizes life areas with Whole Sign houses.

    Are the planet positions fetched from the internet?

    No. The page calculates the Sun and planet positions in its own JavaScript engine using JPL's approximate orbital elements for 1800–2050. A compact periodic model is used for the Moon. No astrology API receives your sign choices at runtime.

    Are the scores luck percentages?

    No. Love, career, money, communication and energy scores are not scientific probabilities or luck percentages. They summarize supportive and challenging themes inside the rule-based astrology model on a 0–100 scale.

    Is astrology scientifically proven?

    No. Astrology is not scientifically established as a predictive method. This tool calculates astronomical positions; the personal meaning assigned to those positions is astrological and intended for entertainment and reflection.

    How precise are the astronomical positions?

    The tool uses JPL's approximate 1800–2050 planetary model plus a compact lunar model for a daily reading. This is suitable for daily theme calculation, but near an exact sign ingress or retrograde/direct station it can differ slightly from a high-precision ephemeris; professional natal-chart precision is not claimed.
