Cookie Policy

This policy describes Hesapica’s necessary storage and optional analytics and advertising technologies, including how you can change your preferences.

Necessary storage

Necessary storage supports core site functions and remembers your privacy choice. Hesapica’s consent manager stores its current preference record under the localStorage key hesapica_cookie_preferences_v2. Necessary storage cannot be disabled through the site preference panel.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is only initialized through Hesapica’s consent layer when the analytics category is allowed. Its purpose is to measure page and calculator use so we can understand what is useful and improve the site.

Advertising and marketing

Manual Google AdSense ad requests are tied to the advertising/marketing preference. The AdSense framework may load earlier under denied ad consent so a certified Google CMP/TCF message can operate where required; this does not by itself mean a manual ad unit has been requested.

Google Consent Mode v2

The consent manager maps choices to Google consent states including analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization. Before a decision, optional states are initialized as denied and are updated after the user makes a choice.

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

For regions where Google requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF for AdSense, Hesapica’s own preference panel does not replace that certified CMP. Google’s published CMP or another certified solution must remain active where required.

How to change your choice

On the first visit you can accept all optional categories, keep only necessary storage, or open detailed preferences. Later, use the Cookie preferences control in the footer or the centrally added preference entry to change your decision.

Browser controls

You can also remove or block cookies and site storage using browser settings. Doing so may reset saved calculator values and privacy choices.

Updates and contact

This policy may change when services, technical architecture or applicable requirements change. Questions can be sent to hesapicaofficial@gmail.com.
