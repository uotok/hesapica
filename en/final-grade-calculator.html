Final score scenarios

This tool calculates weighted-grade math only. Your school may also apply minimum-final rules, letter-grade thresholds, curves or other policies.

How do I calculate what I need on my final?

Quick mode uses your current average excluding the final, the final exam weight and your target course grade.

required final = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight

For example, with a current grade of 68, a 40% final and a target of 70, you need about 73 on the final.

When should I use detailed mode?

Use detailed mode when exams, projects, quizzes or assignments have different weights. The calculator verifies that total weight is 100% and solves the remaining final requirement.

Interpret the result correctly

  • If the required score is above 100, the target is mathematically unreachable on a standard 0–100 final scale with the current weights.
  • If the required score is 0 or below, the target grade is already mathematically secured even with a zero on the final.
  • The result does not include school-specific minimum-final rules, curves or grading policies.

Frequently asked questions

How is the required final grade calculated?

The calculator uses your current weighted contribution and the final exam weight to solve the score needed to reach your target course grade.

Do component and final weights have to total 100%?

Yes. In detailed mode, all entered component weights plus the final exam weight must total 100%.

What if the required final score is above 100?

The target grade is mathematically unreachable on a 0–100 final scale with the current grades and weights.

Does this include grading curves or minimum-final rules?

No. Check any course-specific minimum-final, relative grading, curve and letter-grade rules separately.
