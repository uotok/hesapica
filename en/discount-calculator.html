How is the discounted price calculated?

First, the percentage discount is calculated from the original price. Any fixed coupon is then subtracted from the remaining price, capped so the final price cannot fall below zero. Total savings are the original price minus the final price.

A headline such as “20% off” does not always tell the whole story. Fixed coupons can materially change the effective discount, so this tool shows both effects together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the effective discount rate show?

It is total savings divided by the original price. When a percentage discount and fixed coupon are combined, it shows the true overall discount on the original price.

Can a coupon make the final price negative?

No. The coupon is capped at the price remaining after the percentage discount, so the calculator never produces a negative final price.

Can I compare offers from different stores?

Yes. Enter each store’s original price, discount rate and coupon separately to compare the resulting final price and effective discount.

Does changing currency convert the price?

No. Currency selection changes the display unit and formatting only; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

Is tax included in this discount calculation?

The calculator applies the discount and coupon to the price you enter. If that price includes tax, the result remains tax-inclusive unless the seller applies different checkout rules.
