How do you calculate a tip and split the bill?

The basic tip calculation multiplies the bill amount by the selected percentage. The calculator then adds an optional fixed service fee and divides the final total equally among the number of people.

Tip = bill amount × tip percentage
Final total = bill + tip + service fee
Exact per person = final total ÷ number of people

Quick example

On a $120 bill, an 18% tip is $21.60. With no extra service fee, the final total is $141.60; split four ways, the exact amount per person is $35.40. Changing the currency only changes display formatting, not the arithmetic.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

Not always. A restaurant-added service charge or gratuity may be different from an optional tip. In this calculator the tip percentage applies only to the bill amount; the optional service-fee field is added afterward as a fixed amount. Check the receipt to avoid adding the same charge twice.

What does per-person rounding do?

The round-up option moves each person's exact share up to the selected increment. That can make the amount collected slightly larger than the true total, so the calculator shows the extra amount created by rounding. Leave rounding off when an exact split matters.

Tip presets are shortcuts, not recommendations

Tipping customs vary by country, venue and service type. Preset percentages are only arithmetic shortcuts and do not imply that any particular rate is required or appropriate. For other percentage scenarios, use the Percentage Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tip percentage apply to the service fee?

No. In this calculator the tip percentage applies only to the bill amount; the optional service fee is added afterward.

What if a service charge is already included?

Leave the extra service-fee field blank and decide separately whether any additional tip is appropriate.

How is the bill split?

The final total is divided equally by the number of people.

What does round-up do?

It rounds each person’s contribution upward to the selected increment and shows any extra amount collected.

Are the percentage preset buttons recommendations?

No. They are only quick arithmetic shortcuts because tipping customs vary by location and context.
