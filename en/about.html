About Hesapica

Learn what Hesapica builds, how the English section is structured and how we approach calculator quality.

What is Hesapica?

Hesapica is a calculator platform designed to make everyday calculations easier to understand and use. The English section focuses only on country-neutral tools for finance, investing, math, health, cars, dates and everyday planning.

Why is the English section global-only?

The Turkish site also contains calculators tied to Turkish taxes, tariffs, social-security rules, education systems and local institutions. We intentionally do not mix those tools into the English homepage. This keeps the English experience relevant to a global audience and avoids presenting local rules as if they applied everywhere.

Our approach

Inputs, results and explanations are kept as clear as practical. Calculators state their method and assumptions, validate common edge cases and are designed to work comfortably on phones, tablets and desktop screens.

How we treat calculator results

A useful calculator should do more than display one number. Where practical, Hesapica also shows supporting metrics, formulas, limitations and related scenarios so users can understand what drives the result.

Continuous improvement

We review tools when we find bugs, receive useful feedback or identify clearer ways to explain a calculation. The platform can evolve as formulas, browser capabilities and user needs change.

Important note

Hesapica provides general information and planning estimates. For high-impact financial, legal, tax or health decisions, verify relevant figures with current official sources or a qualified professional.
