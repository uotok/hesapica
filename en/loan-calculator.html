What does this calculator do?

A borrowing decision is more than one monthly-payment number. This page combines three related workflows:

  • Loan mode: estimates monthly payment, total interest, optional interest levies and upfront costs.
  • Installment mode: compares an installment plan with an optional cash price and shows payment pressure.
  • Early-payoff mode: estimates a payoff amount from the remaining principal, accrued interest and any lender-provided extra charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are a loan payment and an installment purchase the same thing?

The payment math can be similar, but loan mode also lets you model optional interest levies, origination fees and insurance. Installment mode focuses more on down payment, financing rate, extra fees and the difference from a cash price.

Will the early-payoff estimate exactly match my lender’s official payoff quote?

No. This tool provides an estimate. Your lender’s current payoff quote can include day-specific interest, contract terms, fees or other adjustments, so use the official quote for a final decision.

Why is monthly income optional?

Income helps interpret payment pressure, but the core payment and cost calculations still work without it.

Important: Results are estimates for planning. Rates, fees, taxes, payment schedules and payoff rules vary by lender and jurisdiction. Use your lender’s official disclosures, amortization schedule and payoff quote for a final decision.
