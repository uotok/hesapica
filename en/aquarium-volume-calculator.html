Calculation method

Rectangle: inside length × inside width × actual water depth ÷ 1000 = litres. Cylinder: π × (inside diameter ÷ 2)² × actual water depth ÷ 1000 = litres.

Displacement defaults to zero; a user-supplied estimate reduces the geometric volume. The selected change percentage is applied to the estimated usable volume.

Source: Merck Veterinary Manual — Management of Aquarium Fish. Merck Veterinary Manual.

Important notes

  • Use the actual water depth and inside dimensions: outside measurements can overestimate volume.
  • Monitor water chemistry, temperature, ammonia and nitrite; volume alone cannot indicate tank health.
  • The water-change percentage is your input, not a care recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate a safe number of fish?

No. Species, filtration, biological load, swimming space and water quality all matter.

What water-change percentage is best?

There is no universal number. Select a percentage based on your animals and measured water quality; the calculator only computes the volume.
